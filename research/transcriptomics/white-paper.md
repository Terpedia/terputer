# Terputer Transcriptomics: an evidence-preserving workspace for terpene RNA-seq and Perturb-seq

**Working white paper · 27 October 2026 · Methods and results from executed Terputer notebooks**

For the fuller biological and statistical background relevant to the Codebreaker Labs role, see the [public transcriptomics white-paper snapshot](../terpene-transcriptomics/manuscript/white-paper.md) and its [claim ledger](../terpene-transcriptomics/manuscript/claim-ledger.md). This note focuses on the Terputer workspace and its executed examples. The public snapshot preserves the source manuscript and evidence files at [research.terpedia.com](https://research.terpedia.com/terpene-transcriptomics/).

## Abstract

Terputer's repository combines a Codespaces Jupyter kernel, research chat, public-study discovery, Terpedia knowledge search, and curated transcriptomics study and sample records in Terpedia BigQuery. A larger ten-study snapshot is available through the remote MCP while its warehouse load remains pending. The live chat backend has not yet received the newer Terputer API-key endpoints, so this paper does not claim an end-to-end member-authenticated chat run. We used the notebook workflow to examine two terpene-exposure bulk RNA-seq studies and a public Perturb-seq summary, then reviewed separate single-cell extensions. One **within-study descriptive comparison** in *Botrytis cinerea* GSE138291 found that 1,8-cineole and terpinen-4-ol response vectors had Spearman correlation 0.433 across 9,000 control-expressed features, with 64.8% sign agreement. This does not establish a shared causal mechanism. A linalool dataset in *Listeria monocytogenes* showed broad expression changes, but its processed FPKM values and unresolved gene crosswalk prevent formal differential-expression or cross-organism pathway claims. In the published A549 genetic Perturb-seq summary, the only shared targets across the two selected interferon contexts were BATF2 and PARP12, and their feature-wise correlations were near zero (0.069 and 0.038). These genetic perturbations demonstrate context analysis, not terpene exposure. **The central question—what metabolic or signaling response is reproducible across independent terpene exposures—remains open.**

## Research question and decision rule

> Which metabolic and signaling responses to terpene exposure are reproducible, and which depend on cellular context or a specific target?

A reproducible response needs the same mapped feature or pathway measured in independent biological experiments, with compatible chemical identity, dose, time, control, assay, annotation, and direction of effect. A target-specific claim additionally needs target engagement or perturbation evidence. We do not promote a processed abundance ratio, a shared-control correlation, or a genetic Perturb-seq association to either claim.

## Why this matters for computational biology

The [Codebreaker Labs computational biologist role](https://codebreakerlabs.io/careers/computational-biologist) calls for reasoning about pooled perturbations, guide/variant assignment, donor and batch structure, cell state, and predictive models in primary-cell single-cell RNA-seq. Terputer addresses the upstream research workflow: find studies, preserve sample and chemical provenance, run an inspectable notebook, and separate observation from inference. For variant-installation data, the same workspace should later record edit genotype, guide assignment quality, donor, batch, cell state, and per-cell QC before any pseudobulk or hierarchical effect model. No HDR variant-installation data were analyzed here.

## Methods

### Study selection

We selected [GSE136998](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE136998) for bacterial linalool exposure (three treated, three controls), [GSE138291](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE138291) for a five-arm fungal exposure experiment (three samples per arm: untreated, tea tree oil, 1,8-cineole, terpinen-4-ol, and a two-component mixture), and two additional studies, [PRJNA608617](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/browser/view/PRJNA608617) and [PRJNA913446](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/browser/view/PRJNA913446), with selected sample metadata and separate publisher-table audits. Their raw reads have not been reprocessed. [PIE Jiang](https://huggingface.co/datasets/arcinstitute/PIE_jiang) A549 IFN-β and IFN-γ differential-expression summaries were used only to demonstrate a genetic perturbation-by-context comparison. These are different biological questions and are not pooled into a terpene meta-analysis.

### Calculations

For each processed FPKM feature, we computed `log2((mean treated FPKM + 0.1)/(mean control FPKM + 0.1))`. GSE138291 arm similarity used Spearman correlation, sign agreement, and median absolute ratio difference on features with mean control FPKM ≥ 1. We varied the pseudocount (0.01, 0.1, 1) and left one control sample out in a sensitivity screen. The PIE comparison intersected shared perturbation targets and compared published feature-level effect summaries after the notebook's filtering. All derived tables are in `research/transcriptomics/results/`. No p-values, false-discovery estimates, guide reassignment, or raw-read reprocessing were generated.

DHA is an artemisinin-derived sesquiterpene drug, so it broadens the chemical and organism contexts considered here. The project compares one library per condition and does not support an independent-replication or target-dependence conclusion.

### Paclitaxel response across two single-cell contexts

We added a [checksum-pinned A2780 analysis](gse162256/README.md) of [GSE162256](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE162256), with an [executed notebook](../../notebooks/terputer-gse162256-single-cell.ipynb) , [marker table](gse162256/results/marker_descriptive.tsv), and [feature-wide background table](gse162256/results/background_descriptive.tsv). Its 9,664-feature processed matrix contains 338 quality-filtered cells across control, paclitaxel, celecoxib, and combination arms. The analysis uses the control and pure paclitaxel arms only; it makes no p-value calculation from cells. All six predeclared cell-cycle genes (MKI67, TOP2A, CCNB1, UBE2C, CDK1, CENPF) have lower A2780 medians at both 16 and 48 hours than control. Among 9,276 features detected in at least 20% of controls, 69.8% and 64.3% also have lower medians at 16 and 48 hours. The marker directions must be read against this broad downward background; they do not establish a cell-cycle-specific program. In the existing [HCC1143 GSE266934 marker table](gse266934/results/marker_cpm_ratios.tsv), MKI67, TOP2A, CDK1, and CENPF have negative descriptive ratios at 24 hours, whereas CCNB1 and UBE2C have positive ratios at both 24 and 72 hours. This is a **directional disagreement for two markers**, not a formal context interaction. HCC1143 used 1 nM paclitaxel at 24/72 hours; A2780 used 5 nM at 16/48 hours. Cell line, timing, dose, platform, and reported expression scale all differ. The interferon panel does not establish cross-context agreement: IFIT3, MX1, and OAS1 are absent from the A2780 processed matrix, and the remaining A2780 marker medians are mixed or zero-heavy.

## What can be answered now

**Within one fungal context**, pure 1,8-cineole and terpinen-4-ol have partially aligned descriptive response vectors. The separate PfMYST × DHA analysis has concordant gene-rank patterns across its own time and glucosamine strata, but one library per condition. **Across independent contexts or targets**, the present evidence is insufficient to name a reproducible metabolic or signaling program. GSE136998 has unresolved gene identifiers for pathway mapping; GSE138291 is fungal and shares its controls; PIE describes genetic perturbation under interferon, not chemical exposure. It would be scientifically misleading to list “universal terpene pathways” from these materials.

## Next analyses

1. Obtain raw counts or quantify reads for GSE136998, GSE138291, PRJNA608617, and PRJNA913446 with versioned references and sample-level QC. Verify the exposure metadata and chemical identity against the associated papers.
2. Estimate within-study effects using count-aware models and the recorded design, then apply stable organism-specific gene-to-pathway mappings. Preserve gene-level tables, confidence intervals, and multiple-testing corrections.
3. Compare independent studies at pathway level only after verifying comparable dose and time strata. Use a hierarchical model or leave-one-study-out validation to quantify cross-study agreement and heterogeneity.
4. For any chemical-plus-Perturb-seq experiment, include dose, vehicle, guide identity, assignment confidence, cell state, donor, batch, replicate, and target-engagement evidence. Aggregate cells to biological replicates or use an appropriate mixed model; never treat cells as independent biological replicates.
5. Extend the loaded study and sample metadata with reviewed contrasts and analysis artifacts in Terpedia BigQuery. The proposed schema includes `transcriptomic_studies`, `transcriptomic_samples`, `transcriptomic_contrasts`, `transcriptomic_observations`, and `transcriptomic_gene_effects`. The gene-effect loader requires curated study and contrast records plus source and execution receipts; those rows remain unloaded while BigQuery access is denied. Ontology mappings, biological-replicate linkage, and fuller analysis-run lineage remain future work.

## Limitations and reproducibility status

This is a working research white paper based on executed descriptive notebook analysis. It is not a peer-reviewed mechanistic conclusion. GSE136998, GSE138291, and GSE242559 inputs are processed FPKM, so formal differential expression cannot be inferred from the reported ratios. Cross-species gene and pathway mapping has not been done. The selected PIE tables do not contain terpene-exposure effects and cannot answer target specificity. The PRJNA cohorts now have bounded publisher-table audits, while HepG2 has a separate executed raw-count reanalysis and UFH-001 raw-count processing remains in progress. Previously loaded BigQuery study/sample metadata may not include the newly prepared Penicillium, Fusarium, GSE263903, or GSE242559 evidence; ten-study metadata, 17 contrasts, and 54 observations remain staged locally until the curated loader is run with Google access. The GSE263903 menthol-mint arm is a nicotine-containing mixed-aerosol exposure with pooled mouse libraries and collection date confounding, not a purified-terpene or target-specific result. GSE242559 directly applies menthol in a cell line, but its processed-FPKM factorial audit does not establish TRPM8 dependency or replication with the aerosol study. The remote MCP serves a pinned public snapshot, and the earlier Cloud Run adapter supports the last verified read-only metadata queries; the personal-key chat and MCP flow remains undeployed and has not been demonstrated end to end.
