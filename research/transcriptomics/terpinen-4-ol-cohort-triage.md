## Why these cohorts matter

GSE157638 adds another exact terpinen-4-ol exposure to compare with the existing *Botrytis cinerea* GSE138291 arm. Their organisms, time points, media, dose scales, and transcript annotations differ. A cross-species comparison requires independently estimated within-study effects and a versioned orthology or pathway mapping; similar named pathways in two papers would remain insufficient.

PRJNA729806 is relevant to the target-dependence question because its article describes an RNAi susceptibility result. The three currently exposed sequence runs alone do not permit a new exposure contrast, and the phenotype does not distinguish detoxification, tolerance, and a direct molecular target. PRJNA808823 could supply an independent same-species, same-compound cohort if its six reported libraries become accessible; its 24-hour adult fumigation should still be compared with the other beetle study only after dose, stage, and treatment design are reconciled.

## Planned Terputer work

1. The [GSE157638 FPKM audit](https://github.com/Terpedia/terpedia-mrsa-terpinen4ol-fpkm) is executed with pinned GEO inputs, sample QC, and descriptive ratios.
2. Retrieve the raw GSE157638 runs and reference annotation, estimate integer gene counts, and fit a model with biological replicates. Keep this inference separate from the FPKM audit.
3. Revisit GSE138291 with raw counts and compare the terpinen-4-ol arm against its own untreated controls. Report context heterogeneity only after mapping comparable features and checking uncertainty.
4. Trace PRJNA729806 control accessions and the RNAi phenotype design, and locate the six PRJNA808823 runs or an accessible count matrix. Until then, retain both beetle studies as publisher-claim leads rather than reanalyzed cohorts.
