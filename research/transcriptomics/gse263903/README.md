# GSE263903: menthol-mint aerosol single-cell curation

The [primary GEO series](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE263903) has ten mouse-lung 10x libraries across air, PG:VG, mango, menthol-mint, and tobacco aerosol arms. `build_sample_manifest.py` retrieved the ten sample labels, sex fields, treatment labels, and collection dates from GEO on 26 October 2026. The [source paper](https://elifesciences.org/articles/106380) states that each sex-labeled library pools two mice and that flavored e-liquids contain tobacco-derived nicotine.

The [forkable executed analysis](https://github.com/Terpedia/terpedia-menthol-aerosol-single-cell/tree/7ca77dcc01687fbdb38169059f127480fc0b8082) examines all ten libraries. `source_manifest.tsv` pins the ten 10x matrix URLs and SHA-256 hashes. The copied `sample_qc.tsv`, `contrast_summary.tsv`, and `analysis_summary.json` are snapshots from that project commit. `stage_curated_rows.py` maps this bounded evidence into the four JSONL files under `research/transcriptomics/bigquery-ready/`.

The staged collection contains all ten sample metadata records, four descriptive menthol-mint contrasts (against PG:VG, air, mango, and tobacco), and 17 selected numerical observations. The contrasts use an exploratory myeloid-marker bin and eleven targeted genes. They are **not** differential-expression estimates. Nicotine, other flavor constituents, aerosol metals, sex pooling, cell capture, and collection date prevent a menthol-specific or target-specific inference. The metadata records are staged for BigQuery and are also served by Terputer's commit-pinned public snapshot after its revision is updated; staging alone does not imply a successful BigQuery load.
