# GSE125511: β-caryophyllene dose response in hypoxic UFH-001 cells

**Archive inspection: 26 October 2026. Status: processed-matrix audit executed.** The [forkable project](https://github.com/Terpedia/terpedia-ufh001-bcp-fpkm) includes the executed notebook, pinned inputs, one-to-one HGNC crosswalk, result tables, and claim-linked report. This public cohort offers a second human cell-line response to the exact compound β-caryophyllene. It is bulk RNA-seq, not single-cell or genetic Perturb-seq. The [GEO series](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE125511) identifies three UFH-001 triple-negative breast cancer cultures per arm: DMSO vehicle, 20 µM β-caryophyllene, and 200 µM β-caryophyllene. All nine libraries were exposed to 1% O₂ for 16 hours. The [sample manifest](sample_manifest.tsv) records the matrix column, GEO accession, arm, dose, and source record for each library.

The three control samples are explicitly described in their GEO sample records as identical to GSM3514416, GSM3514418, and GSM3514420 from [GSE123856](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE123856). They are **reused libraries**, not a second independent control cohort. Do not count them twice in a cross-study synthesis. GSE125511 itself does not contain a normoxic β-caryophyllene arm, so it cannot estimate a β-caryophyllene-by-oxygen interaction.

## Source and matrix audit

The [GSE125511_FPKM_geneMatrix_NORMALIZED.txt.gz](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE125nnn/GSE125511/suppl/GSE125511_FPKM_geneMatrix_NORMALIZED.txt.gz) download is preserved alongside this triage with SHA-256 `662960a32245e6b64b20879215e42d340ba19a5c6c16a0b79fbef0ef6c88d415`. It has 60,603 unique `tracking_id` rows and nine FPKM columns. All 545,427 sample cells parse as finite, nonnegative numbers; none is blank. Its column order matches the interleaved vehicle/20 µM/200 µM sample order in the GEO records. FPKM is processed abundance, so this file does not independently support a new count-based differential-expression model.

The [original breast-cancer article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8561761/) reports coordinated cholesterol-biosynthesis gene upregulation and increased sensitivity to β-caryophyllene after HMGCR blockade. The [later oxygen/lipid paper](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0281396) analyzes related hypoxia and lipid phenotypes and points to this GEO series. These are **source-reported interpretations and experiments**, not conclusions reproduced by this triage. The apparent HMGCR interaction could reflect pathway compensation or susceptibility; it does not prove direct chemical binding to HMGCR.

## Comparison boundary

GSE276099 supplies a [separate executed HepG2 β-caryophyllene audit](https://github.com/Terpedia/terpedia-hepg2-bcp-rsem) with three ethanol-vehicle and three treated libraries. The two human studies share the named compound but differ in cell line, oxygen, dose, exposure duration, vehicle, library processing, and gene identifier system (Entrez-like numeric IDs versus Ensembl IDs). A cross-study comparison requires a versioned one-to-one identifier map and within-study effect estimates first. Directional agreement would be hypothesis-generating; disagreement cannot be assigned to cell identity alone. An experiment that crosses cell context and exposure under matched conditions is needed for an interaction estimate.
