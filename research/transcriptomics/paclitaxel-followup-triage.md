# Paclitaxel transcriptomics follow-up triage

**26 October 2026 · Metadata review only.** The [A2780 GSE162256](gse162256/README.md) and [HCC1143 GSE266934](gse266934/README.md) analyses are descriptive because cells do not substitute for independent culture replicates. The next dataset should preserve biological-sample identity and a defensible treatment/control contrast. None of the studies below has been expression-analyzed in this project.

| GEO series | Available design and files | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [GSE131984](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE131984) | SUM159 single-cell paclitaxel, DMSO, other drugs and combinations; processed count and metadata text files are available. GEO's treatment protocol mentions triplicate cultures, but the downloadable cell metadata has `nGene`, `nUMI`, and `orig.ident` only; `orig.ident` identifies eight condition/sample labels, with one `Tax` and one `DMSO` label. | **Do not call the single-cell contrast triplicate-replicated.** Culture identity is not recoverable from the inspected metadata. It remains a possible descriptive resistance context, not a clean replication of acute exposure. |
| [GSE266919](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE266919) | The GEO design describes 78 tumor biopsies from 44 TNBC patients with paclitaxel, nab-paclitaxel, and anti-PD-L1 combinations; some samples have pre/post labels. Processed cell-type RDS files are large, and the series mixes clinical scRNA-seq with other assays. | **Candidate for donor-aware analysis**, conditional on matching each biopsy to patient, regimen, biopsy time, and available counts. Paclitaxel and nab-paclitaxel are distinct formulations; treatment assignment and prior therapy can confound comparisons. Do not pool the arms or treat cells as patients. |
| [GSE230454](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE230454) + [GSE230459](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE230459) | Mouse dorsal-root-ganglion bulk RNA-seq, with four female control and four female paclitaxel sample titles in the [GSE230461](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE230461) SuperSeries. Separate processed TPM spreadsheets are supplied. | **Highest-priority compact replicate-aware candidate** for a *different tissue and species*; verify dose, timing, actual animal independence, and the source's treatment labels before analysis. The paclitaxel series' sample-level `treatment_protocol` text says saline despite the paclitaxel titles, so metadata reconciliation is required. TPM supports exploratory effects but raw counts/reads are needed for formal count-aware inference. |
| [GSE207772](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE207772) | Three bulk RNA-seq samples per PB3 parental or drug-resistant derivative, including a paclitaxel-resistant derivative. | **Exclude from acute exposure replication.** The paclitaxel arm is a selected resistant cell line, while controls are parental cells; treatment history and clone selection are inseparable from acute drug response. Useful only for a separately framed resistance question. |
| [GSE107676](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE107676) | A549 paclitaxel/control RNA-seq with two GEO sample records and processed splicing and expression files. | **Do not use for replicate-level inference** without additional independent-sample evidence. It may provide a descriptive splicing context. |

The next analysis should first reconcile GSE230454/GSE230459 with the associated methods and sample records, then pin the processed-file checksums and record the eight female libraries in a sample manifest. A report should keep its in vivo neuropathy question separate from cancer-cell paclitaxel response and mark gene-level effects as exploratory until count-level data and design are verified.
