# Fusarium proliferatum D-limonene publisher-table audit

The [executed notebook](../../notebooks/terputer-fusarium-proliferatum-dlimonene-supplement.ipynb) audits Tables S3–S5 of the [publisher supplementary workbook](https://static-content.springer.com/esm/art%3A10.1186%2Fs12864-025-12389-w/MediaObjects/12864_2025_12389_MOESM2_ESM.xlsx) for the [Zhou et al. study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12864-025-12389-w). The [claim-linked white paper](generated/white-paper.md), [claim ledger](generated/claim-ledger.tsv), [result tables](results/), and [execution receipt](../execution-receipts/terputer-fusarium-proliferatum-dlimonene-supplement.json) travel with it.

The published differential-expression table has 2,375 unique feature rows: 1,240 down and 1,135 up. The article reports 1,134 upregulated genes. `rrna13` is the one upregulated entry outside the `FPRO_` identifier pattern, but four downregulated entries also have `rrna` identifiers, so the reason for the paper/table mismatch is unresolved. Every Table S4 entry meets the printed adjusted-p and fold-change thresholds. In Table S5, only ribosome (`ko03010`) is below adjusted p 0.05 among H3K9ac-associated KEGG terms; none of the H3K27ac terms is. These are author-processed statistics, not independent RNA-seq or ChIP-seq reanalysis or direct target evidence.

The 331 KB publisher workbook is omitted from Git. Restore its exact bytes from the HTTPS URL and SHA-256 in [report-spec.json](report-spec.json) with `python restore-inputs.py research/fusarium-proliferatum-dlimonene-supplement/report-spec.json` before rerunning. The [PRJNA1234870 RNA-seq reads](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/PRJNA1234870) remain to be reprocessed. The study used three biological replicates per arm at 200 mM for 24 hours, with substantial growth inhibition; its response cannot yet be interpreted as independent replication of the lower-dose *Penicillium* limonene biotransformation study.
