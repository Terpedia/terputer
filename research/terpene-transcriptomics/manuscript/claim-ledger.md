The statistical design, dependency experiment, and model evaluation sections are **proposals**. They do not report newly estimated terpene effects, verified installed variants, raw-cell QC, or target engagement. The status table and prospective replication criteria are interpretations and proposed decision rules, not new quantitative results. The sign-frequency baseline, direct treated-versus-treated contrast, raw count-level fungal model, and cross-study linalool comparison have not yet been executed. Methodological references are enumerated in the white paper's References section; this ledger records the provenance of its study-specific and quantitative claims.
