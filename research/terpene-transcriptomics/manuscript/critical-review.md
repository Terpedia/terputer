# Critical review of white paper v0.9

26 October 2026. This is an author-side scientific audit, not independent peer review. The [claim-to-source ledger](claim-ledger.md) identifies the source and calculation behind the paper's quantitative claims.

## Editorial verdict

The paper is a credible **methods case study and research proposal** for the [Codebreaker Labs computational biologist role](https://codebreakerlabs.io/careers/computational-biologist). It now links four executed processed-expression terpene exposure analyses, three publisher-table audits, and bounded single-cell and genetic-perturbation examples. It still does not identify a reproducible terpene pathway, estimate a terpene-by-context interaction, prove a direct terpene target, or demonstrate installed-variant pooled-HDR analysis. The [MRSA notebook](https://github.com/Terpedia/terpedia-mrsa-terpinen4ol-fpkm/blob/main/notebooks/terputer-mrsa-terpinen4ol-fpkm.ipynb) improves same-compound study selection, and the [HepG2 notebook](https://github.com/Terpedia/terpedia-hepg2-bcp-rsem/blob/main/notebooks/terputer-hepg2-bcp-rsem.ipynb) adds one human cell-line context. Neither changes those conclusions.

## Major findings, ranked by consequence

| Priority | Finding | Consequence | Required next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cross-study reproducibility remains unestimated.** GSE138291 (*Botrytis*) and GSE157638 (MRSA) both include terpinen-4-ol, but differ in organism and exposure context. Their available notebook results are processed FPKM summaries, without a shared feature or pathway model. | A named metabolic or signaling program cannot yet be called reproduced or context dependent. | Reanalyze both studies independently from counts or reads, version orthology or pathway mappings, then estimate comparable effects and heterogeneity with biological-replicate uncertainty. |
| 2 | **The beetle RNAi result is a source-reported functional lead, not a direct target result.** The primary article reports greater terpinen-4-ol mortality after CYP9Z6 or GSTs7 RNAi. Its PRJNA729806 archive exposes only three treated RNA-seq runs in the checked public listing; phenotype tables and interaction design have not been independently audited. | Silencing a detoxification or tolerance gene could modify susceptibility without that protein binding the chemical. | Locate expression controls and phenotype tables, verify knockdown and viability controls, estimate the chemical-by-RNAi interaction, and obtain orthogonal target-engagement or rescue evidence. |
| 3 | **A second beetle paper's declared public data were not retrievable.** The lncRNA study reports six fumigation/control libraries and BioProject PRJNA808823, but the checked NCBI BioProject-to-SRA link query and ENA report returned no runs. | Its source-reported lncRNA and pathway findings cannot yet be independently reanalyzed or used as replication. | Obtain run accessions or a count matrix from the authors or archive; preserve the 24-hour adult fumigation design separately from the other beetle experiment. |
| 4 | **The human HepG2 lead is bounded by processed fractional counts and one cell line.** GSE276099 has a six-library β-caryophyllene contrast, but 75,830 retained values are fractional and 20 matrix rows have blank sample cells. Its per-sample dose fields also disagree with the common 150 µM protocol. The notebook finds a descriptive PGF decrease; the article reports a PGF overexpression phenotype. | Neither a new 433-DEG replication nor a β-caryophyllene-by-target interaction or direct PGF binding follows from the current audit. | Resolve dose metadata, reprocess original reads with a pinned reference, and use matched genetic and chemical perturbations in more than one context. |
| 5 | **The fungal arm similarity statistic shares a control.** GSE138291 1,8-cineole and terpinen-4-ol log2-ratio vectors use the same untreated mean. | Spearman 0.433 and 64.8% sign agreement can partly reflect denominator covariance; neither demonstrates independent replication. | Fit covariance-aware arm contrasts, report the direct treated-arm comparison, and compare sign agreement with a marginal-frequency baseline. |
| 6 | **Processed FPKM and publisher tables limit inference.** The MRSA audit's 248 higher and 518 lower twofold thresholds change to 243 and 480 with a larger pseudocount. The published fungal DEG and KEGG tables were audited, not refitted from reads. | Descriptive thresholds and author-computed statistics cannot be combined as if they were one calibrated effect-estimation pipeline. | Reprocess raw reads with frozen reference/annotation releases, inspect sample independence and batch, and fit replicate-aware models before pathway synthesis. |
| 7 | **The single-cell and employer-assay bridge remains conceptual.** Paclitaxel examples do not isolate a cell-line interaction, and the processed A549 Perturb-seq join is not terpene exposure. No installed-variant pooled-HDR data, donor-aware effects, or edit-state verification are analyzed. | The paper supports study design and computational judgment, not a completed variant-functional-genomics result. | Execute an assay-matched dataset analysis with raw cells, guide or edit assignments, independent biological units, and a held-out prediction evaluation. |
| 8 | **Publication and service provenance are distributed.** Projects, notebooks, reports, and the central manuscript span repositories. The Cloudflare MCP can serve a pinned six-study snapshot, while the expanded BigQuery export is staged and member-key introspection is not live. | A public report link or tool index does not prove a member can run the complete chat-to-analysis pipeline. | Keep immutable links and receipts, load curated records with a Google principal, deploy the chat backend, and verify member-authenticated MCP calls. |

## Claim assessment

| Claim | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Four chemical-exposure processed matrices and three publisher-table audits have been executed | Supported by linked notebooks, result artifacts, and receipts. Scientific interpretation remains bounded by their source classes. |
| GSE157638 has 2,925 FPKM feature rows and pseudocount-sensitive group-mean ratios | Supported as a checksum-pinned descriptive calculation. It is not a new DEG or pathway analysis. |
| GSE276099 has 17,034 retained numeric-gene rows and a descriptive PGF decrease | Supported by the checksum-pinned HepG2 notebook and result tables. Fractional processed counts do not reproduce the source DEG model or establish PGF target engagement. |
| A metabolic or signaling response reproduces across terpene exposures | Unsupported by the analyzed contrasts. |
| A response depends on a specific direct chemical target | Unsupported. A source-reported beetle RNAi susceptibility phenotype is a candidate functional lead, not a binding or independently audited dependency result. |
| A549 IFN-β/IFN-γ genetic signatures have low concordance for BATF2 and PARP12 | Supported for the processed, filtered summaries only; not a replicate-aware context interaction. |
| The project demonstrates pooled HDR variant Perturb-seq analysis | Unsupported. The variant section is a design and evaluation proposal. |
| Terpedia members can use chat to run this research end to end | Not yet verified live: BigQuery expansion and backend deployment remain pending, and member key introspection returns 404. |

## Publication decision

Publish v0.9 as a **working, critically audited white paper** beside its ledger and executed notebooks. For a job application, describe the source-pinned notebooks, report pipeline, and evidence triage as demonstrated work; describe cross-study pathway replication, chemical-genetic mechanism, and pooled-HDR predictive modeling as next research steps. A biological finding requires raw-count analysis, comparable study design, uncertainty, and external scientific review.
